Stratospheric Payload Thermal Analysis: 60-Minute Dwell at 40 km, 30 W, Cu-ETP Straps

Study: balloon-ttp/transient-0-40km-dwell-60min-etp-copper · Date: 2026-08-06 · Prepared by: agent-cfd-fea-engineer

1. Executive Summary

This study continues the completed Cu-ETP-copper ascent (balloon-ttp/transient-0-40km-etp-copper) for a further 3600 s (60 min) held at a fixed 40 000 m altitude, starting from that study's actual solved t = 9600 s field — mirroring the OFHC dwell study (balloon-ttp/transient-0-40km-dwell-60min) so the two can be compared directly at both the ascent endpoint and after an additional hour.

Result: the ETP-vs-OFHC gap shrinks further over the dwell, it does not grow. At t = 13200 s, jetson_module reaches 78.183 °C and interface_plate reaches 78.925 °C, against the OFHC dwell's own solved 78.152 °C and 78.894 °C — a delta of +0.031 K, smaller than the +0.06 K delta observed at the ascent's own endpoint (t = 9600 s). The two straps track the same pattern (+0.031/+0.033 K). Radiator temperatures are essentially unchanged (−0.026/−0.027 K, i.e. slightly cooler, within numerical noise). Total radiative rejection at t = 13200 s is 26.33 W against 30.1 W injected — both material choices show the same ~3.8 W shortfall, down from the ~6 W shortfall at the ascent's end, indicating the assembly is approaching (but has not reached) a quasi- steady state at 40 km for either strap material. Restart continuity from the ETP ascent's own solved state was confirmed to within 0.045 K.

As hypothesised in the ascent study's report, the strap material's influence stays small because the bolted-joint contact resistances (1.58 K/W each) dominate the die-to-radiator path — and here, an additional hour at fixed conditions actually lets the small conduction-resistance difference matter less, not more, as the whole assembly's temperature field settles toward its (still-rising) quasi-steady profile. For this hardware, Cu-ETP remains thermally indistinguishable from OFHC copper across both the ascent and an extended hold at altitude.

9. Validation Methodology

  • Energy balance: injected heat flux(es) should match the specified load(s) at every checkpoint; external flux at any adiabatic patch should integrate to 0 W.
  • Interface balance: each coupled component-to-component interface should agree between the two sides reporting it, within a few percent.
  • Restart continuity (transient studies only): no temperature discontinuity at a segment restart boundary greater than ~1 K.
  • Physical plausibility: no region colder than the coldest applicable sink temperature or implausibly hot given the load and materials.
  • Not performed unless noted above: mesh independence; benchmark against measured hardware data.
  • Energy balance: jetson_heat_source = 30.1 W throughout; every styrofoam_box and other component-wall external patch integrates to 0.0 W (adiabatic confirmed).
  • Interface balance: every coupled interface in cht_kpis.json's interfaces_W agrees between its two reporting sides (e.g. copper_strap_left_to_radiator_bracket_left −13.89 W vs radiator_bracket_left_to_copper_strap_left +13.89 W).
  • Restart continuity within this study: verified across both internal segment boundaries (10800 s, 12000 s) via run_summary.json's per-segment final_time and the log's Time = sequence — no discontinuity.
  • Restart continuity from the source ETP ascent study: the first post-restart sample (t = 9620 s: jetson_module 71.379 °C) matches the ETP ascent's own final solved value (71.334 °C at t = 9600 s) to within 0.045 K — well inside the ~0.3 K gate.
  • Physical plausibility: coldest region (radiator_plate_2, 0.295 °C min) well above the ~−65 °C sky-sink floor; hottest region (interface_plate, 78.9 °C max) well under any plausible hardware limit.
  • Data-integrity note specific to this run: an earlier automated attempt at this study (via the unattended cfd-qwen driver) silently skipped copying in the ETP ascent's solved t=9600s state and instead solved a full, invalid run from a fresh 20 °C initial condition. That invalid case state, and the postProcessing/.../0/ data it produced, was identified before any of it was analyzed or reported, and was discarded; the study was then completed correctly (verified restart continuity above) before any of these results were generated. See agent-data/lessons.md.
  • Not performed: mesh independence (inherited limitation, mesh unchanged from the ascent studies).

10. Conclusions & Recommendations

  1. Cu-ETP remains a viable substitute for OFHC copper over an extended hold at altitude, not just at the ascent's endpoint. The peak-temperature penalty actually shrank from +0.06 K (t=9600s) to +0.031 K (t=13200s) — the opposite of what a naive "resistance compounds over time" intuition might suggest. This is consistent with the die-to-radiator path being joint-resistance-dominated (§3.3): as the assembly's temperature field settles toward its quasi-steady profile, the small conductivity difference between the two coppers becomes proportionally even less significant.
  2. Neither material choice reaches steady state within 60 minutes at 40 km. Both the OFHC and ETP dwell studies show ~26.3 W rejected against 30.1 W injected — the ~3.8 W shortfall (down from ~6 W at the ascent's end) is still charging thermal mass. A longer dwell would be needed to observe true convergence, for either material.
  3. No further action is needed on the strap material question. Across both the ascent and this dwell continuation, Cu-ETP and OFHC copper are thermally indistinguishable for this hardware within the precision this analysis supports. Re-run only if the bolted-joint TCR estimates (the actual dominant uncertainty) are replaced with measured values.
  4. Process note: this study's final result required discarding one invalid unattended-agent run (see the data-integrity note above) — the driver's automated safeguards (loop-guard, known-warning injection) correctly handled three earlier failure modes on the sibling ascent study, but a fourth (a silently skipped required step, not caught by the model's own stated verification gate) was only caught by a human/Claude review of the smoke-test's n_steps value before it could propagate into the full 3-segment solve.
